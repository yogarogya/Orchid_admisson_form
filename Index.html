Guidelines & Norms
  1. Admission to Orchid University is provisional and subject to verification of original documents.
  2. The University reserves the right to cancel admission if any information submitted by the student is found to be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.
  3. The student shall abide by all rules, regulations, policies, and code of conduct prescribed by Orchid University from time to time.
  4. The University reserves the right to modify academic schedules, examination patterns, curriculum structure, and institutional policies whenever required.
  5. Students must maintain minimum attendance as prescribed by the University and statutory authorities.
  6. Any act of indiscipline, misconduct, ragging, or violation of University rules may lead to disciplinary action, including suspension or cancellation of admission.
  7. Students are responsible for regularly checking official communication shared through email, portal, SMS, or other University channels.
  8. Orchid University shall not be responsible for any loss of personal belongings within the campus premises.
  9. Submission of false documents or suppression of facts may lead to cancellation of admission at any stage.
  10. The University reserves the right to withhold results, certificates, or academic records in case of non-payment of dues or disciplinary matters.
  11. Students enrolled under industry-integrated programs must also comply with the policies and regulations of the respective industry partner.
  12. In case of withdrawal of admission, the candidate shall not be eligible for any refund from the University, unless otherwise specified under the University refund policy.
  13. No candidate shall be allowed to pursue two regular/full-time courses simultaneously during the same academic year.
  14. The University shall not be responsible for any delay in postal transit or non-receipt of Admission Letter, Welcome Letter, Interview Letter, Intimation Letter, or any other admission-related communication.
  15. All disputes, if any, shall be subject to the jurisdiction applicable to Orchid University.
  16. The University reserves the flexibility to modify or reassign the student's training/work location, industry partner, or study location or Department, whenever required, in accordance with academic planning and operational considerations.
